Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

ONN Technologies ("ONN", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This notice explains how we handle personal information collected through our website and business enquiries. We aim to process personal information in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), where applicable.

1. Information we collect

When you use our enquiry form or contact us, we may receive your name, organisation, work email address, telephone number, service interests and message, as well as information you choose to share in later correspondence. Our website also uses Google Analytics 4, which may collect device, browser, interaction and approximate location information, and online identifiers such as cookies, subject to browser settings and applicable consent requirements.

2. Why we process information

  • To respond to enquiries, arrange consultations or demonstrations and prepare proposals.
  • To provide requested services, manage client relationships and fulfil agreements.
  • To understand website use and improve our content and services.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations.

Our grounds for processing depend on the circumstances and may include your consent, steps needed to enter or fulfil a contract, legal obligations or other lawful grounds permitted by POPIA.

3. Enquiry form and service providers

Our website enquiry form uses FormSubmit to process submissions and deliver them to our business email inbox. We also use Google Analytics 4 for website measurement. These third-party services may process information on our behalf in accordance with their own applicable terms and policies. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics may use cookies or related technologies to measure website traffic and interactions. You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Where consent is required for optional analytics technologies, we will need to obtain it before using them. Our current website implementation should be reviewed to ensure the appropriate consent controls are in place.

5. Sharing and international transfers

We may share information with service providers supporting our website, communication and delivery of services, or where required by law. Some providers may process or store information outside South Africa. Where cross-border transfers occur, we seek to apply appropriate protections consistent with POPIA.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the personal information we process. No internet-based service is entirely risk-free. We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for its purpose, legitimate business needs or applicable legal requirements, after which we delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it where appropriate.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of your personal information, object to certain processing, request deletion where permitted, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator.

8. Children

Our services and website are directed at organisations and business users. We do not intentionally solicit personal information from children through our enquiry form.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy to reflect changes to our website, services or applicable law. The effective date above indicates the most recent version.
