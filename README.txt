ORN Technologies Website - Contact Form Update

What changed
- The contact form no longer opens Outlook or another local email app.
- Enquiries are submitted through FormSubmit and delivered to info@orntechnologies.co.za.
- The visitor is returned to the ORN Technologies website with a success message after submitting.
- All form fields now include names so the enquiry details are included in the email.

IMPORTANT - one-time activation
After uploading index.html to GitHub and the site is live, submit the contact form once. FormSubmit will send an activation/confirmation email to info@orntechnologies.co.za. Open that email and confirm the form. After confirmation, future website enquiries will be delivered directly to the inbox.

Upload to GitHub
